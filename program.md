# Program

ReNeuIR is colocated with [SIGIR 2025 in Padua, Italy](https://sigir2025.dei.unipd.it/) and takes place on July 17th, 2025.  All times in the table below are according to the local time zone.

| Time          | Agenda                                                                    |
|---------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 09:00 - 09:15 | Welcome and Introduction                                                  |
| 09:15 - 10:00 | Keynote by [Djoerd Hiemstra](/invited_speakers)                           |
| 10:00 - 10:30 | Shared Task Initiative and Collaborative Experiments                      |
| 10:30 - 11:00 | Coffee Break                                                              |
| 11:00 - 12:30 | Paper presentations                                                       |
| 12:30 - 13:30 | Lunch Break                                                               |

## Detailed Schedule for the Session on the Shared Task Initiative and Collaborative Experiments (11:15 - 15:30)

We will have two talks (10 minutes + 5 minutes QA):

- Resource Tracking in IR Experiments
- A Proposal for an LSR-Benchmark

## Detailed Schedule for the First Paper Presentation Session (11:00 - 12:30)

We have two long papers and one short paper and one invited paper:
- Harnessing Pairwise Ranking Prompting Through Sample-Efficient Ranking Distillation (10 minutes + 5 minutes QA)
- Drowning in Documents: Consequences of Scaling Reranker Inference (20 minutes + 10 minutes QA)
- ColPruner: Combining Complementary Pruning Approaches for ColBERT in Web Search (20 minutes + 10 minutes QA)
- From Vector Representations to Neural Representations: Learned Query-Specific Relevance Functions (10 minutes + 5 minutes QA)

## Detailed Schedule for the Second Paper Presentation Session (14:15 - 15:30)

We have four invited papers (each with 10 minutes + 5 minutes QA):

- 2D Matryoshka Training for Information Retrieval.
- Search Efficiency for Score-at-a-Time Retrieval.
- Efficient Approximate Nearest Neighbor Search on a Raspberry Pi.
- TITE: Token-Independent Text Encoder for Information Retrieval.

## Collab-a-thon Session (16:00 - 17:30)

Inspired by the [collab-a-thon editions at ECIR](https://ecir2025.eu/collab-a-thon/) 2024 and 2025, we intend to conclude the ReNeuIR workshop with an similar initiative. The goal is to inspire new collaborations, explore fresh research ideas, and build lasting connections with fellow researchers that attended the workshop.
