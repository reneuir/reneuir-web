## Collab-a-thon Session (16:00 - 17:30)

Inspired by the [collab-a-thon editions at ECIR](https://ecir2025.eu/collab-a-thon/) 2024 and 2025, we intend to conclude the ReNeuIR workshop with an similar initiative. The goal is to inspire new collaborations, explore fresh research ideas, and build lasting connections with fellow researchers that attended the workshop.
